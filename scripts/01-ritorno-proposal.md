# RITORNO — Proposal Script

**Brand:** Gianna Bellucci
**Format:** 9:16 vertical story ad, 1:30 (90 s)
**Style:** cinematic, natural light, warm Italian palette, burned-in subtitles, first-person female voiceover (same structure as the reference ad — see `../reference/coffee-ad-contact-sheet.jpg`)
**Platforms:** Meta (Reels / Stories / Feed), TikTok
**Status:** DRAFT v1 — for approval

---

## Logline

Eight years in, Giulia has become invisible — to her boyfriend Marco, and worse, to herself. One small, private decision brings her spark back. And Marco can't look away.

## The angle (important)

The story is **not** "she wasn't pretty enough, so she bought lingerie for him."
It is: **she stopped feeling like herself → she does something just for herself → her confidence changes the whole room → he falls for her again.**

Why:
- It matches the brand promise — *"Designed by women who know women… makes everyday moments irresistibly confident."*
- Women (the buyers) root for her and don't feel judged.
- Meta rejects ads that imply negative self-image or put down how someone looks. Framing it around confidence instead of "unattractive" keeps the ad approvable.

## Characters

| | |
|---|---|
| **GIULIA** (32) | Our narrator. Beautiful but running on autopilot — oversized hoodie, hair in a clip, always tired. Warm, a little self-ironic. |

| **ELENA** (55) | Giulia's Italian aunt / older friend. Elegant, direct, funny. The "mentor" (same role as the trainer/friend in the reference). |

## Script

> VO = Giulia's voiceover (burned-in subtitles). Timecodes are targets.

### ACT 1 — The distance (0:00–0:25)

**1. HOOK — 0:00–0:04**
*Close-up, night, bedroom. Giulia lies awake facing the camera. Behind her, out of focus, Marco sleeps turned away, lit by his phone.*
**VO:** "Eight years. And one night I realized he hadn't really *looked* at me in months."

**2. 0:04–0:09**
*Morning kitchen. Giulia in a stretched-out hoodie walks past Marco. He doesn't look up from his laptop.*
GIULIA: "Coffee?"
MARCO: "Mm-hm."
**VO:** "I wasn't angry with him."

**3. 0:09–0:15**
*Restaurant, his friend's birthday. Marco laughs with friends. Giulia sits slightly outside the circle, cardigan buttoned to the top. A beautiful woman at the bar catches Marco's eye — for one second.*
**VO:** "I was angry with myself. Somewhere along the way, I stopped feeling like a woman."

**4. 0:15–0:25**
*Bathroom mirror. Giulia pulls off her hoodie. Underneath: faded, mismatched, practical underwear. She looks for a second, then switches the light off.*
**VO:** "I didn't want to see her. So I stopped looking."

### ACT 2 — The turn (0:25–0:50)

**5. 0:25–0:33**
*Sunny terrace. Elena — silk blouse, red lipstick, gold earrings — pours two espressos. Giulia, in the same hoodie, half-smiles.*
GIULIA: "He doesn't see me anymore."
ELENA *(sips)*: "Cara… do *you*?"

**6. 0:33–0:40**
*Close on Elena, warm, a little cheeky.*
ELENA: "Beauty isn't for him. You put it on in the morning — for yourself. Then everyone else notices."
*She slides her phone across the table: the Gianna Bellucci site.*

**7. 0:40–0:50**
*Night, sofa, Giulia alone. Phone screen: Gianna Bellucci product page. Her thumb hovers… taps "Add to cart."*
**VO:** "It felt silly. It was just lingerie. But it was the first thing in years I'd bought only for me."

### ACT 3 — Ritorno (0:50–1:20)

**8. PRODUCT REVEAL — 0:50–0:58**
*Morning light. The Gianna Bellucci box on the bed. Slow, tactile macro shots: the ribbon, tissue paper, fabric moving through her fingers, lace detail.*
**VO:** "The moment I put it on… something came back."

**9. MIRROR (MATCHES SCENE 4) — 0:58–1:05**
*Same mirror, same bathroom — this time in daylight. Shot tastefully, shoulders up / back view: Giulia in Gianna Bellucci. She doesn't switch the light off. She holds her own gaze. A small smile.*
**VO:** "Her."

**10. 1:05–1:12**
*Montage, three quick shots, the lingerie hidden under her clothes: she lets her hair down · fitted dress instead of the hoodie · red lipstick, Elena's shade.*
**VO:** "Nobody else could see it. But I walked differently. I *felt* different."

**11. HE NOTICES — 1:12–1:20**
*Evening. Marco comes home, scrolling his phone, keys in hand. He looks up — and stops. Giulia, barefoot in the kitchen in the dress, pouring wine, completely at ease. He puts the phone face-down on the counter.*
MARCO *(quiet)*: "…Wow."
GIULIA *(not looking up, smiling)*: "Coffee?"
*He laughs — the old laugh. He crosses the room and pulls her close.*

### PAYOFF + CTA (1:20–1:30)

**12. 1:20–1:25**
*Close two-shot, soft warm light. He looks at her the way he used to. Fade as he kisses her shoulder.*
**VO:** "He came back. But first… I did."

**13. END CARD — 1:25–1:30**
*Gianna Bellucci box + lingerie on silk sheets, logo.*
SUPER: **GIANNA BELLUCCI**
SUPER: *Designed by women who know women.*
CTA button: **Shop now** / *Find your fit*

## Production notes

- **Look:** like the reference — cinematic AI/live-action hybrid, shallow depth of field, natural window light. Act 1 cool and desaturated → Act 3 warm golden. The colour shift does the storytelling.
- **Mirror rhyme:** Scenes 4 and 9 use the same framing, so the before/after lands without needing words.
- **Product:** shown full only in Scenes 8, 9 and 13. Elsewhere it's a secret under her clothes; that secret is the emotional point.
- **Meta safety:** no nudity, nothing sexual, lingerie shown in a fashion / mirror context, no body-comparison language.
- **Music:** sparse piano in Act 1 → warm Italian strings swell at Scene 8.
- **Localisation:** the store runs in 30+ languages, so the VO and subtitles can be re-recorded per market (IT / DE / CZ / SK etc.) on top of the same picture.
