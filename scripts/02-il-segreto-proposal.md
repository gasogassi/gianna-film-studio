# IL SEGRETO DI NONNA — Proposal Script

**Brand:** Gianna Bellucci
**Format:** 9:16 vertical story ad, 1:30
**Tone:** warm, funny, then quietly emotional. Italian family comedy meets a coming-of-age moment.
**Status:** DRAFT v1, for approval · **Locked:** Puglia setting · red Palmira hero product · ElevenLabs v4 voices (Turbo or standard v4, chosen by A/B test)

---

## Logline

Sofia is about to have the biggest day of her life and is falling apart. Her 78-year-old nonna Rosa, the most effortlessly elegant woman in Puglia, finally tells her the secret she's kept for sixty years.

## Why this one

- **No man in the story.** Confidence passes from woman to woman. That's the slogan, *Designed by women who know women*, taken literally.
- **Not about pleasing anyone.** The lingerie is Rosa's private ritual: "something beautiful underneath, just for you." It's Meta-safe and has no body-shaming angle.
- **Different from Ritorno.** It's daylight, comedic, multi-generational and set in the countryside (a Puglian masseria and a whitewashed town), with a different emotional engine: inheritance instead of romance.
- **Reach.** It works for daughters, mothers and the gift-buying audience, so it's a natural Christmas or Mother's Day campaign.

## Characters

| | |
|---|---|
| **SOFIA** (29) | Architect, smart and anxious. Tomorrow she pitches her first big project. Lives in hoodies, bites her nails. |
| **NONNA ROSA** (78) | Silver bob, red lipstick at breakfast, gold rings, linen dresses. Sharp tongue, warm heart, iconic. |
| **MAMMA LUCIA** (54) | Cameo. Rolls her eyes: "Here we go…" |

**Voices:** a single narrator, Sofia (ElevenLabs v3), for continuity. Characters speak in their own performed voices (Seedance dialogue). The comparison render now in progress will show whether that holds up.

## Script

### ACT 1 — The panic (0:00–0:22)

**1. HOOK — 0:00–0:04**
*Night. Sofia's childhood bedroom at her grandmother's house. She paces in a hoodie, laptop open, slides glowing. She bites her nail.*
**VO (Sofia):** "Tomorrow I present the biggest project of my life… and I feel like a little girl playing dress-up."

**2. 0:04–0:09**
*Mirror. She holds up a sharp blazer, then throws it on the bed. Tries another. Throws it.*
**VO:** "Nothing felt like *me*."

**3. 0:09–0:14**
*The door creaks. NONNA ROSA leans in the doorway in a silk robe with red lipstick at 11 pm, holding two tiny glasses of limoncello.*
ROSA: "Amore. Nobody sleeps in this house when you walk like an elephant."

**4. 0:14–0:22**
*They sit on the bed. Sofia slumps. Rosa studies her like a tailor.*
SOFIA: "Nonna, how are you always so… *you*?"
ROSA *(sips, a long pause, a sly smile)*: "You want the secret?"

### ACT 2 — The secret (0:22–0:55)

**5. 0:22–0:30**
*Rosa's bedroom. She opens an antique wardrobe. Behind the linen dresses is a drawer lined with tissue paper. Slow push-in.*
**VO:** "My grandmother had a drawer nobody was allowed to open."

**6. 0:30–0:36**
*Inside: beautifully folded lingerie, decades of it, lace and silk, each piece wrapped like jewellery. Sofia's jaw drops.*
SOFIA: "NONNA!"
ROSA *(unbothered)*: "What? You think I was born seventy-eight?"

**7. 0:36–0:46**
*Close on Rosa, tender now, touching a lace edge.*
ROSA: "Every morning, something beautiful underneath. Not for a man. Not for anyone. For me. Nobody sees it… but everybody feels it."

**8. 0:46–0:50**
*MAMMA LUCIA passes in the hallway with a glass of water, rolls her eyes.*
LUCIA: "Mamma, not the drawer speech again…"
ROSA *(without turning)*: "And look how your mother turned out. Magnificent."

**9. 0:50–0:55**
*Rosa takes out a new black Gianna Bellucci box with a satin ribbon and places it in Sofia's hands.*
ROSA: "I was saving this for your wedding. But this is more important."

### ACT 3 — Il giorno (0:55–1:22)

**10. 0:55–1:02**
*Morning light. Sofia unties the ribbon. Macro shots of lace and tissue. She smiles despite herself.* (Product hero: Gianna Bellucci set.)
**VO:** "It was ridiculous. Nobody would ever see it."

**11. 1:02–1:08**
*Mirror again, the same framing as Scene 2. This time the blazer goes on and stays on. Shoulders back. She doesn't bite her nail.*
**VO:** "But I walked out of that house like my grandmother."

**12. 1:08–1:16**
*Montage in the city, Bari Vecchia: heels on white stone. A boardroom door opens. Sofia presents, calm and owning the room. A client nods.*
**VO:** "I don't remember a single slide."

**13. 1:16–1:22**
*Masseria terrace, evening. Sofia comes home and drops her bag. Rosa raises two limoncellos.*
SOFIA: "We got it."
ROSA: "Of course you got it." *(beat)* "Which colour?"
*They both burst out laughing.*

### PAYOFF + END CARD (1:22–1:30)

**14. 1:22–1:26**
*Close: Sofia's hand and Rosa's hand clink glasses. Rosa's gold rings and Sofia's first ring.*
**VO:** "Some secrets you keep. The best ones… you pass on."

**15. END CARD — 1:26–1:30**
*The Gianna Bellucci box on Rosa's antique wardrobe.*
LOGO: **GIANNA BELLUCCI**
SUPER: *Designed by women who know women.*
CTA: **Shop the gift edit**

## Editing plan (same grammar as Ritorno)

- **Mirror rhyme:** Scene 2 and Scene 11 share identical framing, so the before and after need no words.
- **L-cut:** Rosa's "Nobody sees it… but everybody feels it" carries over the cut to the box in Scene 9.
- **J-cut:** Lucia's eye-roll line starts over Rosa's close-up before we cut to the hallway.
- **Cut on action:** the ribbon pull goes into the lace macro, and the heel step goes into the boardroom door.
- **Comedy timing:** hold a beat on reactions (Sofia's "NONNA!", Lucia's eye-roll). Don't cut early.

## Production notes (lessons from Ritorno)

- **Character turnaround sheets** for Sofia, Rosa and Lucia before any keyframes, with locked wardrobe: Sofia in a hoodie, then a blazer; Rosa in a silk robe, then linen.
- **Anatomy and physics QA** on every keyframe and every clip before cutting: hands, glasses, pouring limoncello.
- **Sound:** constant low score with no ducking, three or four discrete Foley moments (wardrobe creak, tissue paper, glass clink, heels on stone), no noise beds, and a lossless mix with a single AAC encode.
- **Look:** Act 1 in warm tungsten night, Act 2 in golden lamp light, Act 3 in bright Puglian white-stone daylight.
- **Product:** the hero set to be decided. Black and gold suits Rosa's taste, or the red Palmira for continuity with Ritorno.

## Cut-downs

- **15 s:** Scenes 6 ("NONNA!" / "You think I was born seventy-eight?"), 7, 11, 15
